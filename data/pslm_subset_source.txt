DVTE — pslm_hh_subset.csv / pslm_person_subset.csv
Stratified household sample from the full cleaned PSLM 2019-20
  district-level microdata (pslm_hh.csv / pslm_person.csv).
Stratified by province x urban/rural x district; target ~12% of
  households per stratum, floored at min(30, all households) so
  every district in the full data remains represented.
Sampling unit is the HOUSEHOLD: every person in a sampled
  household is included, never a person-level subsample.
Random seed: 20260913 (set seed, fixed for reproducibility).
Built: 14 Sep 2026
This is a TEACHING subset for fast download. For real analysis
  requiring full sample precision, use pslm_hh.csv / pslm_person.csv.
